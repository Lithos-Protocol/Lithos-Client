Outstanding permanent fees

Permanent fees are your share of the swap fees the pool collects. Unlike the liquidity itself, they are not exposed to impermanent loss — once accrued they are owed to you at a fixed amount, whatever the price does afterwards.

Earned is everything your provisions have accrued in the pool. Claimable is the part the vault has actually been handed and can pay out today. The difference is waiting on a flush.
