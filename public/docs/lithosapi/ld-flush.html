Flush pending fees to the vault

POST 
/dex/flush

Move the pool's pending fees into the vault, making them claimable. Anyone may call it and it pays the caller nothing. Returns 400 when nothing is pending. The body is optional.

Request​

Responses​

  • 200
  • 400
  • 409
  • 422
  • 503
  • default

Result of the flush transaction

Nothing pending to flush.

The pool or vault moved. Re-read and try again.

The wallet cannot cover the network fee.

Node or wallet unavailable. Retry.
