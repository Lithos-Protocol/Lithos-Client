SigmaFi bonds

Advanced

Assumes you know Ergo's UTXO model and what a token is. Start with Mining on Lithos if not.

SigmaFi bonds are peer-to-peer loans on Ergo, backed by collateral. A borrower locks collateral, a lender lends ERG or a token against it, and the borrower repays a fixed amount by a fixed block. If they do not, the collateral goes to the lender. This page explains how a bond runs, how to borrow and lend from the web panel, and what your client does with bonds on its own.

It does not cover the API endpoints behind the panel, which are in the API reference under SigmaFi Bonds, or the settings for liquidating bonds, which are in Batching and orders.

What a bond is​

Every SigmaFi bond today is a zero-coupon bond. It pays no interest along the way: the borrower repays one amount, larger than the loan, at the end. A bond has these terms:

TermMeaning
LoanERG, or an amount of one token
RepaymentWhat the borrower pays back, in the same asset as the loan
MaturityThe block by which the borrower must repay. Repayment closes at this block
CollateralERG and any tokens the bond holds until it is repaid or liquidated
Batching feeThe most of the collateral's ERG whoever liquidates the bond may keep
LiquidationThe rule for when the bond can be liquidated. Today, anyone from maturity on

The difference between the repayment and the loan is the lender's return.

How a bond is made​

Either side can start:

You want toYou createThe other side
BorrowAn order, holding your collateral and the terms you ask forA lender fills it. The loan comes to you and your collateral moves into the bond
LendAn offer, holding the loan and the terms you ask forA borrower closes it by posting the collateral you asked for, and takes the loan

An order can fix its maturity to a block, or ask for a term counted from the block a lender fills it. A term counted from the fill does not shrink while the order waits. It must be at least 30 blocks.

Until it is filled or closed, the person who created an order or offer can cancel it and take back what it holds.

The bond asset​

Filling an order or closing an offer mints one token to the lender: the bond asset. Whoever holds it is owed the bond. You can send or sell it like any token.

Its name, SigmaFi ZC-Bond, and its description are set by whoever minted it, so they prove nothing. Anyone can mint a token with the same name. The web panel and Wallet check each bond asset against the chain and mark it ZC-Bond only when it is the asset its bond was created with. A token named like a bond asset that fails the check is marked Not a ZC-Bond.

How a bond ends​

A bond ends in one of two ways, and either way the lender's side goes to a vault for the bond asset's holder:

  1. Repaid. Any block before maturity, the borrower pays the repayment into the vault, and the collateral returns to them.
  2. Liquidated. From maturity on, anyone can liquidate the bond. The collateral goes to the vault, less the batching fee, which the liquidator keeps.

The holder of the bond asset then claims everything the vault holds for it. The claim burns the bond asset, so every vault box for it is claimed in one transaction.

You do not need to liquidate bonds yourself. Lithos miners liquidate every matured bond that pays a batching fee of at least 0.05 ERG, in their own blocks first and by broadcast two blocks later.

Borrow or lend from the web panel​

Open Extensions > SigmaFi Bonds in the web panel. It has four views:

ViewWhat it doesNeeds your API key
BookLists orders you can lend into, offers you can borrow from, and live bondsOnly to send
CreateMakes an order to borrow or an offer to lendOnly to send
PortfolioShows your orders, offers, loans and bond assets, with cancel, repay, liquidate and claimYes
How it worksA short summary of this pageNo

Every action is checked by your client first and shows what you pay, what you receive and any warning before anything is signed. A check never signs or sends anything. The send uses your client's wallet and pays the 0.001 ERG network fee.

Read the batching fee before you lend. If the borrower does not repay, you receive the collateral less up to the batching fee. Orders and offers made from your client carry at least 0.05 ERG, so Lithos miners liquidate them. A bond paying less is left to you to liquidate from Portfolio.

What can go wrong​

SituationWhat happensWhat to do
The borrower misses maturityThe bond can be liquidated. The collateral goes to the vaultClaim it from Portfolio once it is liquidated
The collateral is worth less than the loan at maturityThe borrower may let the bond be liquidatedChoose collateral and terms that cover your loan
An order you lent into had a fee under 0.05 ERGNo Lithos miner liquidates itLiquidate it yourself from Portfolio
You send the bond asset awayWhoever holds it is paid instead of youKeep the bond asset until you claim
You claim while a liquidation is still pendingYour client refuses the claimClaim after the liquidation confirms
