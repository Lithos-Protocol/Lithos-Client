SigmaFi on the Lithos client

Advanced

Assumes you know Ergo's UTXO model and what a token is, and have read the SigmaFi overview.

SigmaFi bonds are peer-to-peer zero-coupon loans on Ergo, backed by collateral. From 1.0.9, the Lithos client and web panel ship full support for the SigmaFi ZC Standard (zcs1) across two markets (ERG and SigUSD): fixed-term and yield-priced borrow orders, standing lend offers with yield curves, secondary bond and share asks, fractional and Selective/Dutch auction share vaults, collateral lot auctions, and token/NFT wraps.

This page covers what the Lithos client and its web panel add: the panel's views, the limits the client enforces, and the batching and arbitrage your client runs. What each action means for a borrower, lender or trader is on Borrowing, Lending and Trading bonds and shares.

The settings that control ZC tracking, automatic batching and ZC collateral arbitrage are in Batching and orders. The API reference has two SigmaFi sections:

SectionPathsCovers
SigmaFi ZC Standard/sigmafi/zc/Live book reads, rate curves, matching, simulations, fill history, wallet history, and all 19 ZC write actions (with /check previews)
SigmaFi First Generation/sigmafi/old/Legacy first-generation bond reads and wallet actions
First-generation routes moved in 1.0.9

First-generation endpoints are served only under /sigmafi/old/ with no aliases; calling unprefixed /sigmafi/orders or /sigmafi/bonds returns 404. Legacy first-generation bond batching has also been removed: batching.sigmafi now runs only the SigmaFi ZC Standard batcher.

How a ZC Standard bond works​

Every SigmaFi ZC bond is a zero-coupon loan in either ERG or SigUSD. It pays no periodic coupons: the borrower repays one fixed repayment amount by maturity, and the lender's return is the discount between the principal funded and the repayment.

TermMeaning
MarketERG or SigUSD (each contract is pinned per market by exact ErgoTree)
Principal & RepaymentLoan funded now and fixed amount repaid by maturity
Maturity modeFixed block height, Term (blocks counted from fill), or Window (lender chooses a maturity within the borrower's min/max range)
PricingFixed principal, or Yield-priced (PricedOrder / PricedAsk) whose principal moves along a yield curve over time
CollateralERG and any tokens locked in the bond until repayment or liquidation
Liquidation ruleMaturity (liquidatable from maturity on) or Oracle (also liquidatable early when ERG/SigUSD cover falls below the bond's collateral ratio, checked against the ERG/USD v2 oracle)
Fill / Sale guardOptional contract-enforced entry guard requiring minimum collateral cover and a fresh oracle at fill time
Fee reserveERG locked in the bond above the collateral to pay a liquidator (default 0.05 ERG); returned to the bond holder on repayment

Filling an order mints three units of the bond asset (using INPUTS(0)'s box id): two units stay locked inside the bond (and later inside its BondVault settlement box), and one circulating unit goes to the lender (or straight into a share mint request). Whoever holds the circulating unit, or the shares minted against it, owns the claim on settlement.

Limits on every ZC box​

The Ergo node and the ZC contracts set the following limits. The client refuses a write that breaks one before it signs anything:

LimitWhat it means for you
30 collateral tokensAn order, a Dutch share vault and a share purchase hold at most 30 distinct tokens. The panel's token picker stops at 30.
4,096 bytes per boxThe node refuses a larger box. A lend offer is refused if the box its first fill leaves would pass 4,096 bytes.
Box minimum per byteEvery box holds at least the node's minimum for its size. The client sizes the boxes it builds to that minimum.

Closing a loan early (Extinguish)​

A live Bond supports five spend paths: Repay, Recollateralize, Liquidate, EIP-39 Refresh, and Extinguish.

If a borrower buys back (or otherwise holds) the circulating bond unit for their own loan, they can extinguish the bond directly (POST /sigmafi/zc/bonds/{assetId}/extinguish + /check, or the Extinguish button in Portfolio and on the bond's terms page):

  • Direct 3-unit burn: Spends the live bond together with the borrower's circulating unit (and an authorizing input at the borrower's script, which the unit box itself satisfies when held at that address) and burns all 3 units of the bond asset directly on the bond, skipping BondVault entirely.
  • Valid at any height: Works before, at, or after maturity (even if the bond is currently liquidatable, though it races any competing liquidation transaction). No oracle read and no full face-value repayment are required.
  • Full release: Returns the locked collateral ERG, collateral tokens, and the lender's liquidation fee reserve straight back to the borrower's wallet (less the miner fee).
  • Spotting your own debt on the market: Market reads (asks, orderbook, curves, auctions, search, lookup, portfolio) expose a borrowedByMe flag whenever a listed bond unit, share lot, or sale-auction lot backs a live bond this wallet borrowed for. In the web panel these listings are marked Your loan with a Buy back action.
    • Buying from a PricedAsk requires the live bond as a data input, so buyback and extinguish run as two transactions: buy the unit first, then extinguish from Portfolio or the bond page.
    • If the bond was split into fractional shares, acquiring all 100 shares (100,000,000 raw units at the default share count) lets you Un-fractionalize (final-claim before pull) to recover the bond unit and then extinguish.

Web panel flows​

Open Extensions > SigmaFi in the navbar. The top bar switches between the SigUSD and ERG markets, toggles Days / Blocks, displays the live block height and ERG/USD oracle status, and provides quick lookup (/) and the Create menu:

ViewWhat it does
MarketInteractive rate curve (bid envelope from standing lend offers vs ask dots/line), order book, fill history chart, and sidebar order ticket with quote, APR path, oracle price-shock simulator, and Your loan buyback tags
AuctionsOpen collateral Lot Auctions and Dutch Auction Vaults (floor and excess auctions) with live falling prices and direct buy previews
PortfolioYour active borrowing positions (repay, recollateralize, extinguish), lending positions (bonds, shares, wraps, claimable settlements, un-fractionalize), open orders/offers/asks/lots/requests (cancel), cost basis, P&L, and wallet activity
SearchLook up any bond asset, share token, wrap receipt, offer, or box id, with on-chain origin verification and settlement history

Every write action in the panel runs through a /check preview first. The preview shows exact ERG and token deltas, fees, and warnings before anything is signed.

The ERG/USD oracle is refreshed often, and a refresh waits in the mempool for a block. While exactly one refresh is pending, the panel shows the oracle as refreshing at the new price, and quotes and cover checks use that price. A write that reads the oracle is sent at once only when miners are sure to include the refresh first. Otherwise the client answers held and sends the write once the refresh confirms, checking its quote again first. A held write is kept in memory only: restarting the client drops it. Two competing refreshes pause oracle writes until the next block.

Creating positions (Create menu)​

ActionContract(s)What it posts
BorrowOrder / PricedOrderLocks collateral with fixed, term, or window maturity, optional yield-curve pricing, optional fill guard, and maturity or oracle liquidation rule
LendLendOfferStanding liquidity box with a yield curve (up to 20 points), collateral floor, oracle-age limit, and optional automatic delivery into a fractional or Selective/Dutch share mint
Sell a bondSaleOrder / PricedAskLists a circulating bond unit or share lot on the secondary market at a fixed price or yield curve, supporting partial fills on shares
Sale auctionLotAuctionSells a basket of collateral tokens (and ERG on a SigUSD lot) whole at a linear falling price from opening to floor
Split into sharesFractionalMint / DutchAuctionMintLocks one bond unit to mint fungible shares (100,000,000 by default) backed by a FractionalVault or DutchAuctionVault
Wrap an NFTWrapMint → WrapVaultWraps a single token (such as a LithosDex liquidity position NFT) into a clean 1-unit receipt safe to pledge as bond collateral, with an unwrap path that burns all 3 receipt units. Costs about 0.038 ERG: two box minimums, a 0.03 ERG refresh reserve, a 0.005 ERG mint fee and the miner fee

Three of these actions carry a cost or an outcome that is easy to miss:

  • An ask's closing fee. An ask box holds 0.001 ERG plus its refund fee. When the ask fills in full, that ERG goes to the buyer or to the batcher that fills it, not back to you. An ERG ask priced at or under that amount pays you nothing.
  • Dutch shares on a SigUSD bond. A Dutch vault auctions everything that is not SigUSD after a liquidation. That includes the 0.001 ERG every box holds, so a SigUSD Dutch vault always runs an auction.
  • Wrap requests from older clients. The WrapMint contract changed in 1.0.9. A wrap request posted by a 1.0.9 beta client uses the old contract, which this client does not track. Post wraps from 1.0.9 or later.

Fractional and Dutch share vaults​

Splitting a bond unit mints N circulating shares (100,000,000 by default) to the owner and locks N + 1 reserve shares alongside the bond unit in a vault:

  • FractionalVault: Once the underlying bond settles into a BondVault, anyone can pull the settlement into the vault (burning all 3 bond units; FractionalVault and DutchAuctionVault both pin BondVault's exact script hash on PULL so an un-settled bond cannot mimic a settlement). Share holders then claim pro-rata ERG and tokens in kind by burning their shares. Holding all outstanding shares before settlement also allows a final claim (Un-fractionalize) to recover the live bond unit.
  • DutchAuctionVault (Selective recovery): When a liquidated bond's settlement is pulled into a Dutch vault, any thin collateral item below the vault's in-kind threshold triggers an automatic falling-price auction:
    • Floor auction (state 1): If currency held is below the floor, the buyer tops up the vault to the target currency amount and takes the whole collateral lot.
    • Excess auction (state 4): If currency already clears the floor, only thin items below the threshold are sold while thick items stay for in-kind pro-rata claims.
    • Once sold (state 2) or repaid (state 0), share holders claim pro-rata proceeds.

Script disclosure (zcs1)​

ZC contracts commit to party addresses (borrower, offer owner, seller) by their 32-byte Blake2b256 script hash. A transaction discloses a named script when one of its outputs carries that script. If no output carries it, the client attaches the script in context variable 64 of INPUTS(0). Inputs do not count: the node lists a pending transaction's inputs without their scripts, so only an output or variable 64 shows the script while the transaction waits in the mempool. Trackers and batchers then resolve the script in one lookup. Boxes whose named script was never disclosed cannot be refunded or matched by batchers and are skipped.

Automatic batching and collateral arbitrage​

Your Lithos client tracks every open ZC box in real time from the blocks your node syncs (plus the mempool overlay for reads) and runs two automated revenue engines on top of it:

  1. ZC Standard batching (batching.sigmafi):

    • Self-funded. Batching never spends your wallet boxes. Every transaction balances protocol inputs to protocol outputs plus a reward output (less the 0.001 ERG miner fee on broadcasts).
    • Actions executed:
      • Offer matches: Runs the deterministic zcs1 matcher across ERG and SigUSD markets, chaining an offer's continuation box across multiple orders or asks in the same block.
      • Packed liquidations, expiry refunds and refreshes: Packs up to 20 independent pieces into one transaction: matured and early-oracle bond liquidations (subject to minBatchingFeeNanoErg, default 0.05 ERG), expired orders/offers/asks/lots, and paid storage-rent refreshes on aged vaults (subject to minActionFeeNanoErg, default 0.002 ERG). Aged bond refreshes are stamped 60 blocks back and packed separately per EIP-39.
      • Standalone actions: Share mints, settlement pulls into share vaults, wrap mints, and aged wrap vault refreshes.
    • Block priority & stats: In your own blocks, transactions run fee-less, supersede competing mempool spends, and send rewards into the rollup holding top-up. Past broadcastAfterBlocks (default 1), uncontested work is also broadcast to the mempool. Statistics attribute all ZC batching revenue under SigmaFi (sigmafi).

  2. ZC collateral arbitrage in your own blocks (batching.arb.zcEnabled):

    • Runs inside the arb stage right after the DEX and ZC batchers, on your existing arb stake box. It can even arbitrage a DutchAuctionVault auction that ZcBatcher opened in the same block.
    • Evaluates Lot Auctions, Dutch Auction Vaults (state 1 floor and state 4 excess), and non-priced Sale Orders across both ERG and SigUSD markets. A bundle acquires SigUSD through a pool first when needed, buys the ZC box, and sells the priced collateral tokens through 1-hop or 2-hop ErgoDEX and LithosDex pools in the same block.
    • Strict profit & stake safety: Every bundle re-verifies on the signed outputs that the exact arb stake returns whole with zero tokens and that net ERG profit clears batching.arb.zcMinProfitNanoErg (default 0.001 ERG).
    • Collateral pricing scope in 1.0.9: Pool-traded fungible tokens are priced. SigUSD is priced at what the SigmaUSD bank pays on a redeem when the bank can take the amount, and by the pools past that. Unpriced tokens (including ZC bond tokens, share tokens, and wrapped NFTs) are valued at 0 ERG. They are swept to your wallet, with the 0.001 ERG box minimum deducted from profit, only if the priced collateral still leaves a net profit.

  3. ZC collateral arbitrage in any block (batching.sigmafi.collateralArb):

    • Your client also broadcasts a purchase to the mempool, so it can buy in blocks other miners find. It needs batching.sigmafi.broadcast = true.
    • Each purchase is one transaction: the ZC box, the sale of its collateral through one ErgoDEX or LithosDex ERG pool, and the ERG from your node wallet that pays for it. The transaction lands whole or not at all, so your wallet never holds the collateral.
    • Only the ERG market qualifies. SigUSD markets, sales that need two pools and baskets of several priced tokens stay in your own blocks.
    • A purchase is sent only when its net profit is at least minReturnPercent of the ERG it pays out (default 10%) and at least minProfitNanoErg (default 0.01 ERG). One transaction takes at most maxErgIn from your wallet (default 10 ERG).

Statistics attribute ZC collateral arbitrage profits, from both paths, under Arb (arb).
